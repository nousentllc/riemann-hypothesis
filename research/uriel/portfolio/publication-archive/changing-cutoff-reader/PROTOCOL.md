# A trained reader facing a changing cutoff

22 November 2026. Recorded before the first fit or measurement of any reader in this experiment. The counting law and the collision between centers 0 and 65 at cutoffs 26 and 64 are known from the preceding assessment. The local hash records the design without an independent timestamp.

**Question.** Can a small supervised reader distinguish a truthful answer from a claim that its record is sufficient to support, when the cutoff is supplied only at read time?

**Population and split.** The terminal cutoff is 64. There are 81 equally weighted designated center residues, 0 through 80. Evaluate each positive weight e up through each cutoff Z=1,…,64. Hold out the entire cutoff blocks 24 through 29 and 59 through 64. All other cutoffs are training cutoffs. All centers appear on both sides; queries within a cutoff never cross the split. Some exact weights are absent from training. Report exhaustive state-query scores on the named finite population, including per-cutoff and per-support-label counts. There is no sampling-based uncertainty or new-system generalization claim.

**Write before the query.** Compare two fixed seven-bit records using exactly the same hidden centers and query cells. The terminal-list tag stores the unique terminal weight, or zero for an empty list (65 values). The residue tag stores the center residue (81 values). The encoder sees no future cutoff or query. Both records presume authenticated binding, an agreed codebook, no extra identifying channel, and seven fixed payload bits; alphabet sizes used differ. The formal control labels a visible observation yes/no only when all compatible centers agree on its truth. Otherwise the licensed action is abstention.

**Models.** Fit separate deterministic axis-aligned CART classifiers by empirical multiclass Gini reduction with state-query frequencies (no class weights), maximum depth 8, no pruning, and no fit on test cutoffs. Resolve split ties by feature order then increasing threshold; split at the lower observed value. Leaf ties prefer abstain, then no, then yes. Skip splits with zero gain. Plain features are the decoded tag, cutoff and query weight. Informed features append the integer cutoff's base-three power band and the capped number (0 through 4) of low ternary digits shared by the decoded tag and the query weight. These are public functions of visible inputs; the zero sentinel is treated numerically as 0. The ternary transform is an explicit arithmetic inductive bias. No feature receives an admissible-set count, a center hidden by compression, a test label, or a precomputed membership flag.

Fit (i) a plain three-action reader (support labels), (ii) an informed three-action reader (support labels), and (iii) an informed binary reader (individual-center truth labels). This crosses the feature and objective comparisons one at a time. The binary task has no abstain action. The support labels used as supervision are generated from compatible *training* truths; test truths and the support oracle are sealed from fitting. An independently coded modular oracle is evaluated against strict integer valuations. Also score fixed always-no/always-abstain baselines and the exact support oracle.

**Predictions.** H1: the terminal tag will force genuine ambiguities on changed cutoffs, even when binary truth accuracy is high. H2: with the same informed features and depth, using support supervision will reduce definite answers on ambiguous test cells relative to training on binary truth. Loss of positive or negative coverage is possible. H3: informed three-action training will improve positive recall relative to plain three-action training on the lossless residue channel. Record all outcomes as stated, including ties or failures; do not tune depth or split after seeing them.

**Controls and metrics.** Store support confusion counts, hidden-truth counts, correct licensed positives and negatives, wrong-signed answers on determinate observations, definite answers on ambiguous observations (including accidentally true ones), per-cutoff results, and relevant denominators. Reorder/rename serialized tags using a fixed nontrivial permutation with the inverse codebook, and verify the paired predictions. Without a trusted binding, substitute valid tags of centers 1 and 2 and compare predictions to the original witness under the original queries. The deliberately corrupted channel has no implicit tamper detector. Check the 0/65 collision at weights 11 and two cutoffs. Keep experiments private and run with CPU and standard-library Python. This tests a learned decision-tree reader of a prescribed record; the memory encoder is fixed.
