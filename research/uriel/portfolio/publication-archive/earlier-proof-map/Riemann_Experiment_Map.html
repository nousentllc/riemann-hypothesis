How to read the evidence and notation
Test, deduction, replay

Finite enumeration can settle an explicitly finite case. A written deduction can connect it to a limit under stated assumptions. “Kernel replay” applies to the Lean declarations identified in that study. This series records no full rebuild of the RH development.

Witness and selection

A witness is a choice that satisfies the proof’s conditions. A selector can guarantee existence while allowing several choices. A construction that passes its score demonstrates one permitted behavior. The original choice can remain unidentified.

Charge and recovery

The odd allowance is a budget for one term. A coefficient relative to the global scale U and the percentage of the odd allowance recovered by g₀ use different denominators. The four bars above use the latter.
