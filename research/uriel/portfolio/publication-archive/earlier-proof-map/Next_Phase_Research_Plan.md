# Can a fresh research agent use what we learned from Lean?

**Decision proposal · 24 November 2026.** The proposed pilot has no model results. Its purpose is to decide whether to retain a specific working method in the Uriel research system. The intervention is an external research aid that a fresh session can receive or retrieve.

The existing reader study established that some model configurations can distinguish settled and unsettled records on two finite panels. Sol High answered both 304-item panels as licensed. The experiment below asks whether providing a method changes what the *same* agent can establish on unfamiliar problems, especially when it must decide which evidence to get next. **Previous reader study** (local archive path: `../flagship-reader-paper/README.md`)

## 1. Develop the method on real mathematics

Return to the original anisotropic binary case, one vector in dimension two. We know the last equation is V=cV. The research task is to trace the two proofs of convergence to V. For each side, identify the quantity being counted, the normalizing measure, the limiting parameter, the common test, and the declaration guaranteeing positive finite volume. Record the first point at which equality of the two limits is justified. Attempt a targeted kernel replay of one substantive step upstream of the final cancellation. Preserve a named hypothesis if its proof remains outside the replay. This produces a useful mathematical result even if the agent intervention later fails. [Starting source](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicSpectrum/Proofs/BlockDecomposition/CountingFormula.lean#L89-L145)

Distill the lesson into a one-page procedure. Specify the claim and the object it concerns. Locate where the decisive fact first enters. Attempt two examples that obey everything observed and disagree on the claim. If both survive, identify a feasible observation that would distinguish them. After obtaining it, update exactly the conclusions it supports. Record the evidence needed to reconstruct the answer after a session reset. Develop the procedure and a length-matched general research checklist before exposing either to evaluation material.

## 2. Test one change on the same model

| Design choice | Proposed implementation |
|---|---|
| Main comparison | One pinned model and configuration. Arm A receives a competent general checklist covering careful reading, verification, citations and uncertainty. Arm B receives the equally long procedure from phase 1. Both see the same output requirements, files, tools, lookup allowance and token budget. |
| First panel | Twelve distinct source families, six from previously untraced formal arguments and six from independent synthetic DC network layouts. Each family supplies two matched records and both arms receive each record in isolated sessions. This gives 48 scored sessions. A lookup can require a second model turn. |
| What changes between records | Change one evidential fact with a known effect on the warranted conclusion. Include both positive and negative determinate answers, and genuinely ambiguous cases. Some are already settled, some need one well-chosen lookup, and some stay ambiguous after any allowed lookup. Balance these across the panel before freeze, and match lookup opportunities within each arm. |
| Freshness | Exclude every worked development family, including the binary proof and its close variants, the old modular panels and the illustrated two-branch circuit. Split by proof family or circuit topology before varying parameters. Strip answer-revealing filenames and annotations. The claim of freshness concerns the study's materials; pretraining exposure to a public theorem is unknown. |
| Record of a session | Log the initial verdict, optional choice of one evidence item from a fixed menu, item returned, final verdict and supporting certificate. The same evidence item produces the same reply in both arms. Each lookup accesses only the study packet. |
| Oracle | For formal items, check the cited proof edge or a replayable derivation in the supplied context. To support underdetermination, exhibit two admissible instances that satisfy the available premises and disagree. For circuits, enumerate all compatible switch states and solve the stated circuit equations over exact rationals. Validate that all scheduled cases have well-defined readings. Keep keys, complete proof contexts and circuit states outside agent access. |
| Administration | Use separate development cases to check for an obvious floor or ceiling and verify that both arms can submit certificates. Freeze cases, keys, scoring, model settings, request budget and randomized run order before the scored panel. Use a deterministic validator where available and treatment-blind review of residual proof judgments. Count unanswered sessions in the denominator and report them separately. |

**Illustration, excluded from scoring.** Two identical switchable branches sit across a known voltage. Each closed branch draws one ampere. A total current of one ampere leaves two possible configurations. A repeated supply-voltage reading leaves both alive. A measurement on the right branch determines whether the left branch is carrying current. This example distinguishes noticing ambiguity, picking an informative observation, and actually updating. Matched cases have precedent in work on evaluating models with small changes to evidence. [Gardner and colleagues, *Evaluating Models’ Local Decision Boundaries via Contrast Sets*](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.117/)

## 3. Decide what the pilot earns

An agent solves a source family when it gives a warranted final verdict **and** a valid certificate on both records. Freeze the strongest attainable verdict with the one-lookup budget for each record. If an available lookup would settle the question, a final abstention after choosing an uninformative lookup earns no family credit, even if it accurately describes what the agent saw. On an inherently ambiguous case, a certificate must give two states compatible with the evidence actually returned. Grade initial claims as well, so a premature assertion followed by a lucky correction remains visible. Report paired family wins, ties and losses, separate results by domain, and list unsupported assertions, missed determinate answers, productive lookups, tokens and time. Count unnecessary abstention on an initially settled case separately from abstention after an unhelpful lookup. Enumerate exact paired uncertainty at the family level; twelve families provide a directional pilot.

| Observed pattern | Decision |
|---|---|
| The method gains at least one net family in each domain; pooled unsupported assertions do not rise and pooled warranted answers on resolvable cases do not fall | Fund a new, independently keyed panel to test retrieval and replication. These are proposed resource-allocation thresholds, to be fixed before scoring. |
| Fewer guesses come with fewer warranted answers | Treat increased caution as the leading explanation. Examine which evidence requests and certificates failed before revising the method. |
| Both arms solve almost everything, time out, or show mixed differences | Publish the paired outcomes and the resulting limit on interpretation. Diagnose difficulty, delivery or compute on separate development cases. Preserve the scored pilot as run. |

If the supplied procedure earns replication, compare a fresh session with the general checklist, one with the procedure in its initial context, and one with that procedure available through the ordinary research archive. Give all three the same archive budget and comparable distractors. Log whether the procedure was found, opened and used. An advantage only with the procedure in front of the agent points to a retrieval or application problem. Replication through ordinary retrieval would justify a final test on an earlier unseen, versioned internal research record: can a later session update the claim affected by a source revision while preserving the claims that still have support?

## Resource order and deliverables

First prepare the binary source trace, both one-page instructions and a small set of disjoint development examples. Build and smoke-test the oracles locally. Then freeze the paired pilot. Count the proposed input, output and optional lookup turns before choosing a priced configuration and a separate hard cap. Preserve the scored packets, arm assignments, receipts, accepted and rejected certificates, and a case-level failure table. The clearest positive result would be a fresh session that finds a decisive fact, changes its conclusion, and can show why that change was warranted in a domain the procedure never mentioned.
