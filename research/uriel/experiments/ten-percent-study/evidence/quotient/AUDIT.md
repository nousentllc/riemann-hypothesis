# Where six gaps on a 64-cycle fail

Take the even residues 0, 2, …, 2t−2. Their neighbors at increments ±1, ±3, ±5 fill t+5 consecutive odd residues, for every 1 ≤ t ≤ 26. Include every other odd residue. There are exactly t+(32−t−5)=27 occupied leaves, each with rate (64/27)δ. None of the six increments joins two occupied leaves. Applying x ↦ ax+b for odd a gives all the tested variants. This is an exact-size-compatible profile with 64/27 below 2.38. A seemingly simpler variant with only one even leaf on 64 residues at rate at most 2.38 fails the root sufficient mark comparison, since its even mean is at most 2.38/64, whereas the paired test at total mean one asks for more than e²/100.

The least label in each dyadic coset fixes its even child. If a nonempty coset has no occupied class in that child, no marked tile on such an orbit can contain its required even singleton. Encode a labeling by bit s−1+c at the node c modulo s, where s is a power of two less than 64. A zero bit selects c modulo 2s for the canonical even child and a one bit selects c+s. All these orientations are realizable by lexicographic traversal in preferred-child order. The experiment compares every mask to this geometric requirement. It then applies exact paired tile inequalities to every remaining node, including the homogeneous leaf condition for deeper levels. These strict sufficient inequalities permit fixed-count sampling to pass the original score via the anchored tile and occupancy estimates. There are 53,248 triples (t,a,b) and 13,312 distinct masks. Orientation zero permits (13,31,25), with gain fraction 100/533 (18.76%) in the profile limit. The integer 5517290612998927258 is an explicit adverse orientation. For every one of the 13,312 masks it has a nonempty node with an empty preferred child. This obstructs the geometry regardless of the chosen positive rates on those leaves. In the prespecified 129-labeling panel, 81 labelings had this geometric obstruction, and only the all-zero labeling yielded a 10% certificate within this family. The remaining observed failures can arise in the sufficient tile comparison or the gain threshold. This experiment does not rule out other 64-cycle constructions.

For the gain calculation, at a node of width w let o be the number of occupied leaves in the odd child and h the number of odd-step residues at that node having no present pair. The ratio for the uniform profile is the sum of 2ho over nodes divided by 27 plus the sum of wo. The additive 27 includes the uniform tail finer than these leaves. As a lower estimate it needs only blocked-step transport; identifying the full limiting template ratio additionally uses abundance of realized pairs on all allowed long-orbit steps. The hand geometry alone cannot identify the mask chosen by Lean.
