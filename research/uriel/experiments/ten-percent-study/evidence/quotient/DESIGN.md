# Six missing directions on a 64-cycle

Test a small dense configuration with odd blocked steps ±1, ±3 and ±5, transported by all affine symmetries. Before any labeling test, fix a run of t consecutive even leaves (spacing two) and retain every odd leaf outside their six-step neighborhood. For 1 ≤ t ≤ 26 there are exactly 27 occupied leaves. The uniform positive rate in units of the selector density is 64/27. This clears the exact-size capacity gate, unlike the analogous four-step interval in the 32-cycle with at most 13 occupied leaves at rate at most 2.4.

At each fixed scale of the dyadic tree a nonempty node must contain a canonically even retained leaf to pass its tile requirements. Evaluate this necessary combinatorial condition first and also the sufficient paired tile thresholds when applicable. Try the all-zero labeling and a prespecified 128 cases generated by Python's Random(19031) over 63-bit orientation words. Record a concrete failed labeling if the template fails there. A failure after enumerating every t, odd multiplier and translation at that labeling refutes only this family as a universal certificate. Exhaust the 64-cycle geometry independently of the sampled orientations. On a successful labeling, use exact integer counts for missing directions and odd exposure.
