# Where the exceptional sets enter a single host

The exceptional sets contain **integer cohomological weights**. The proof uses them to choose a window, uses that window to bound the cohomology of the field specified by the prime pair, and transfers the bound to the chosen host field. The sets disappear from the exported record. The host's `TT` and `T2` instead contain elements of a finite abelian group. The common data at the transport step are the character, page exponent, ordered primes, scale and field-identification proof. ## 1. Produce the two kinds of exceptional sets

Fix $C_t=401$, an admissible `e,t`, a character ψ, a sufficiently large outer scale $X$, and eligible primes $q,d$. In the B-window theorem the threshold precedes `e,t,X,q,d`. The selected window follows them. The height cutoff for exception construction is $Z=\lfloor X^{C_t}\rfloor$. Write M = `AbF.ofChar ψ`, B′ = `Bchi ψ`, and Lℓ = `AbF.cyc (ℓ * ψ.N)`. [Quantifiers and field parameters](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimeSetBoundDataOfPrimitiveChar.lean#L23-L49) · [Character specialization](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimeSetBoundDataOfPrimitiveChar.lean#L145-L154)

* At 2, the cap $R_0(M)$ is selected before the pair. For this $q,d$, `TwoIn` supplies a base number field with `TwoBase M q d` and `ABound 2 R₀`. The choice of field, hence the set from this proof, may depend on the pair. Part A obtains its set at $Z$ and proves a bound uniform in every composite field satisfying the specified predicate. [Two-primary order of choices](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/SecondBound.lean#L36-L46) · [Construction at 2](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/PartA.lean#L24-L47)
* For each odd ℓ in $B'$, the cap $R_\ell (L_\ell )$ and the choice of centers come from a fixed-field bound. Part B can be applied without $q,d$. It obtains a set at the same $Z$ and transports the estimate to every qualifying realization and layer. The odd branch in the general assembly also handles an unrealized field specification by vacuity. [Odd-prime construction](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/PartB.lean#L50-L83) · [Choices of odd caps and fields](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/ThmBabOf.lean#L18-L60)
* Given its ultrametric centers $s_i$, each branch unions the integers $1\le n\le Z$ satisfying $\Vert s_i-n\Vert <\ell ^{-\lfloor \log_\ell  Z\rfloor }$. Each center captures at most one integer. Duplicates coalesce. The proof obtains the centers as existential data of `ABound`. Their number has an upper bound and need not saturate that bound. [Hypotheses on centers](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/SecondBound.lean#L19-L34) · [Finite filtering and cardinality](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/Exc.lean#L7-L42)

The union has size at most $R=R_0(M)+\sum_{\ell \in B'} R_\ell (L_\ell )$. With $\eta =\varepsilon /(2C_K)$, the recursive selector chooses one $W$ such that $X^{1/J}\le W, W^2\le X$, with $J=2^{R+2}(C_t/\eta )^R$. For every $z\in [W,W^2]$, every exceptional weight $n\le z^{C_t}$ satisfies $n\le z^\eta$. Smaller exceptional weights can remain. In the membership branch the proof uses this stronger size bound, `LemmaK` and `SecondBound`. In the complementary branch it uses `FirstBound`. This distinction is why simply discarding the exceptional terms would lose part of the estimate. ## 2. Keep the window through the cohomological sum

`jointWin_of` obtains $W$ from `ThmBab` for the fixed character and pair, then passes that same $W$ **and the full proof that it is a B-window** to `ThmHV`. In the final route, `of_four_alt` supplies the B-window theorem, the odd minus estimate, and the cohomological count inputs. A B-window by itself gives only part of the necessary bound. [Same-W application](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/PrimeSetBoundDataOfPrimitiveChar.lean#L145-L156) · [Joint count theorem](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/HeightAndVanishing/JointWinOfCounts.lean#L25-L33) · [Final inputs and Ct=401](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/HVCore.lean#L18-L22)

`vwinAt_of_joint` takes $t(z)=\lfloor z^{401}\rfloor$, enlarges $J$ to $J'=\max(J,1)$, and combines thresholds. It derives congruence, coprimality, large-prime and logarithmic size conditions from the hypothesis `WinPair` at the outer pair scale $T=X^{1/J'}$. It obtains the same $W$ from the joint theorem and applies the 2-primary, plus and minus estimates to one field $E$ satisfying `IsChiField ψ (e z) q d E`. The result is `VVle` at the chosen height, with bound $\sqrt{\exp(z^{\varepsilon} )}$, on every $z\in [W,W^2]$. This is an upper bound on weighted cohomological lengths, with finiteness. The proof supplies no table of those lengths. ## 3. Keep the pair through the change of scale

For an arbitrary late threshold, `winPairs` first chooses the outer $X$, then obtains $q,d$ from `PairEx` at $T=X^{1/J'}$, and calls the window theorem with exactly this pair. For the field multiplier $c\ge 1$, it uses $k=c^{1/(2+8\varepsilon )}$. The new window starts at $kW$ and ends at $(kW)^{3/2}$. For an $x$ in it, $z=x/k$ lies in $[W,W^2]$. The proof uses `e z`, the pair scale $T$, and the same $q,d$. Since $z\ge k^{400}$, $x^{400}\le z^{401}$. Since $z\le x$, the permitted mass increases. These are the two directions needed for `VVle.mono`. The variable called `Y` in the exported `WinDatum` is $T$, which can differ from the cohomological window start $W$. [Select pair before W](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean#L20-L37) · [Window geometry and rank rescaling](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean#L39-L73) · [Monotonicity at the transported height](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean#L74-L81)

The proof body fixes $q,d$ for this particular late window. Its theorem statement exports **for every $x$ in the window, there exist $Y,q,d,e$**. Later `supplyOfAdmOf` defines the admissible set with the same inner existential and unpacks membership at the selected scale. Thus a downstream proof can use a single consistent witness at its $x$. Its interface gives no canonical pair shared between two independently materialized scales. [Exported quantifiers](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/WinPairs.lean#L5-L13) · [Scale membership](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ShortNorms/supplyOfAdmOf.lean#L55-L68)

At that scale `hW.good` feeds the prime constraints for the selected host, and `hW.bound` supplies the cohomological bound for the same host field. The argument rewrites `hφe,hHq,hHd` into the field identification before evaluating `hW.bound H.E hCF`, then restricts height to `R.t`. The actual `PairRow` is obtained for that host in `hostOfFieldOf` and passed to the construction of `TT`. The rise from `VVle` to `DIVinN` uses the Sha finiteness, summability, rank equality and `LinkN` on the selected family. The resulting inequalities, together with the other design conditions, reach `ChainTied` and hence `CoreAtH`. ## Post-registration finite specialization at conductor one

For the primitive character modulo 1, `ordC=1`. The first eligible prime `pK` is 7. The field `Kstar 7` is the splitting field of $X^2+7$, hence the imaginary quadratic field of discriminant −7. The odd part of the character order is 1, and the least prime coprime to $2\cdot 1\cdot 7$ is `pOne=3`. To compute `hStar`, we enumerated reduced primitive positive binary quadratic forms of discriminant −7. The bounds $|b|\le a\le c$ give $3a^2\le 7$, hence $a=1$. The sign convention on boundary forms leaves only $(1,1,2)$. The classical correspondence with proper ideal classes gives `hStar=1`. The independent field database reports the same discriminant and class number. The −23 control produced three forms including both interior signs. [Pinned definitions](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/StmtFields.lean#L50-L67) · [Field record](https://www.lmfdb.org/NumberField/2.0.7.1) · [Computation](conductor_one.py) · [Output](conductor_one.json)

Consequently the actual prime-index set for this character is `Bchi = insert 3 ((21.primeFactors).erase 2) = {3,7}`. The combined exceptional set has three components, at **2, 3 and 7**, and cap $R_0(M)+R_3(\mathrm{cyc}(3))+R_7(\mathrm{cyc}(7))$. This computes the indices only. The registered finite control used one synthetic odd component. The output here supplies no values for the caps, centers, exceptions, window start or Sha lengths of the arithmetic field. To reproduce the specialization, run `python3 -B conductor_one.py` in this directory.
