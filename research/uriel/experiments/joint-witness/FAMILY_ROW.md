# Recovering a row at the index of the core

**The original design knows a prime row at every sufficiently late index.** The qualification “original design” matters. The `FamP` record synchronizes the values of `q`, `d` and `QS` between indices at a fixed scale, and the exported character class requires its character and cohomological bounds at every late index. Equal primes at two indices alone cannot produce a row for either. The design construction supplies a stronger property called `QDRowsF`. This is already the quantified property needed after the core chooses its index. The two named existential thresholds for boxes and rows allow different choices of their pair scales.
