# A simultaneous test across prime steps

Before running this panel, fix a cyclic group, a single retained set drawn once from an independent product profile, and the canonical least-label orientation. At each even-order step, edges directed from even positions to their immediate odd successors are disjoint on each orbit. Predict that their indicators are independent Bernoulli variables with success probabilities equal to the products of the two endpoint inclusion probabilities. A second implementation will enumerate sets, with no factorization of edge indicators, and compare the exact distributions. Use all orbits of each nonzero step modulo 8, uniform and two parity-dependent profiles, and a deliberately relabeled group. Record an edge-free sparse orbit with a fully charged odd row. As a negative control, correlate the two endpoints to preserve the individual marginals and prevent any complete edge.

At principal conductor one, use this structure to prove simultaneous abundance of even-to-odd edges for all sufficiently long step orbits on one admissible choice of retained set. Check a large symbolic source-scale instance of the union bound by exact integer arithmetic. This checks the probability inequality only. Whether that scale satisfies all source thresholds is outside the experiment. For the incumbent existential witness the abundance event must be separately justified.
