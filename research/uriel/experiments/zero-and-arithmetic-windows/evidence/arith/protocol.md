# Arithmetic-window tests fixed before enumeration
Read original source commit 40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005. All models below are independent exercises of quantifier order or inequalities; they instantiate no original arithmetic window.
1. On two ordinates with two abstract host indices at each, compare separate existence, joint same-index existence at each ordinate, and global constant-index existence. Exhaust 256 predicate tables. Predict strict implications both ways of increasing strength, with exactly 81, 49, and 31 tables passing in that order.
2. For k in 2,3,5,11, compare W=k^3-1,k^3,k^3+1 in the covering inequality and x'=k^400-1,k^400,k^400+1 for the exponent-transfer inequality. Test by exact integer power comparisons. Probe W=k^3 passing coverage and failing cutoff transfer for these k. Include k=1 at the unit boundary.
3. Test the finite-log-window adversary with equal-height triangular spikes and unequal support lengths. Exact triangular areas on the normalized log axis give contrasting integrated and identical pointwise verdicts. Separately test that changing the target observable can invalidate a fixed-family inference. Formal work will take the property of cofinal host supply as a proposition, and will check its conditional implication to exclusion of a zero, including changing local index at fixed y.
