# Predictions fixed before the model runs, 23 November 2026
The criterion and mathematical shapes of the controls were known before these runs. None of the artificial transforms is a fitted zeta zero. The arithmetic variable is y and u=log y.

- Positive-mass response. Fix theta=1/2, beta=3/4, delta=1/2 and angular frequency 2 in log y. Vary the terminal phase of exp(beta u) cos(2u+phi) across the full circle at U=4,8,16,32,64,128,256. Compare positive-lobe integration with the bounded periodic primitive and the analytic worst-phase lower bound. Estimate the log-mass slope on shared-phase and whole-phase-envelope runs. Prediction: every fixed-width multiplicative-power window detects a common asymptotic exponent 3/8, even at the least favorable phase; the signed integral and two endpoints may mislead. Vary beta to theta and below. Flip the sign of a real residue, and shrink the window in logarithmic coordinates to hide a positive lobe. Treat these as assumption ablations.
- Derivative response. For one conjugate pair with the same beta and frequency compare coefficients at the real and vertically shifted centers; retain the one-sided norm-plus-real combination. Compare the observed normalized log growth to the exact pole distance ratio. Use analytic coefficient formulas and an independent real/imaginary representation. Test the negative-real-residue failure and verify the nonnegative term in the one-sided expression numerically where applicable. These are finite demonstrations, not a proof of localization for arbitrary meromorphic functions.
- Pointwise vs mass. Build one continuous train of triangular spikes with normalized heights exp(h n), h=1/4, centers every integer n in log y, half-width exp(-b n)/8. Use b=1/8,1/4,1/2. Exact whole-spike area and clipped endpoint integrals predict exponential, linear, and decaying weighted mass on late windows despite the same observable maxima. Use shifted and integer endpoints and compare the apparent transform pole to the independently derived geometric leading term. A positive spike on each sufficiently late window defeats ANY fixed pointwise comparison constant for this function.
- Arithmetic/prime controls have separate protocols. This experiment does not search for zeros of actual L-functions and cannot establish a statement quantified over all late scales. No application to other research programs is part of this study.
