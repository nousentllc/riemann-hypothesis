# Supplemental delay control
Added after the primary pole/width experiments. For beta=3/4, theta=1/2, delta=1/2, add a tail at T=log 8192 in log coordinates. Use a continuous onset exp(beta(u-T))-exp(-(u-T)) for u>=T and zero earlier. Test finite observations ending at or before T and the behavior at late multiples of T. Compare the nonzero real residue with the transform computed away from its pole and integrate the mass via an independent rule. This construction can agree with any finite observed trace after compact extension of the trace. Its transform is a model, not a Dirichlet L-function.
