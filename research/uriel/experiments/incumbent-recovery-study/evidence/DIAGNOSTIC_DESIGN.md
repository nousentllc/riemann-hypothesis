Independently compute even-to-odd successor counts by direct traversal and by a cross-correlation of the even and odd children of the relevant dyadic coset. Check all 256 masks on a group of order eight at every nonzero step, for natural and scrambled labels. For larger panels, n=4096, delta=1/16, and exact retained count 256. Compare a stratified support in the odd root half, a uniform same-size control, and a control preserving the exact counts separately on the two parity children of the structured orbit but removing the residue restriction within each child. For each compare the fraction of order-n/2 steps with zero directed overlap on that half, the mean, second moment, dyadic Fourier identity and a one-sided upper bound from the variance. Compare the spectral reading of the same mask against pointwise traversal, retaining both directed counts. A positive directional average alone should allow zero overlap on half the tested steps.
