# Buffered extension of the residue control

The first design permits N near 0.525δn. The original conductor-one selector also compares N to δn and takes a set with scalar score at most its uniform-product expectation. Test a buffered fixed-size law. Reserve the canonical root-even half E for sampling at asymptotic rate 1.5δ. In the root-odd half B, use only the two classes modulo 16 through the least labels of B's two children modulo 4. Sample equal counts there at rate approximately 2δ. The total count is the nearest integer to δn.

Use group sizes 32, 64, and 128 with the natural labeling and 17 shuffled labelings fixed by seeds 0 through 16 at each size. Traverse all steps from their actual least representatives to compare mean and even-site inclusion rates with the orbit-type table. Check the inert-step exposure of the reservoir and the two leaves against dyadic rank predictions, including the finite last-step correction. Check both directions of adjacency on the two excluded step residues, the aggregate odd mass, and the exact group-average lower share of 1/128 after including the sign and prime factors in the preceding study.

As a separate finite control, exhaust fixed-size samples in a population of 11 (all counts 1 through 10, pattern lengths up to 7, all binary patterns and anchor inclusion). Compare their probabilities to independent Bernoulli probabilities under the appropriate conditional marginal. The predicted common likelihood-ratio cap for m distinct inspections is M^m/(M)_m, using the remaining population size when conditioning on an anchor. An inequality failure invalidates the transfer argument. These finite controls cannot instantiate the full arithmetic host.
