# A fixed support with a missing set of generator differences

The output of the original host selector records a size interval, coset shares and a weighted bad-orbit budget. On the principal conductor-one family those conditions alone admit masks for which fallback recovery has normalized lower limit at least **1/16**. This argument holds the arithmetic host, its numeric label order, constants and prime steps fixed before proposing a witness to nonnecessity. It does not reveal the mask used by the original proof. This version centers size at 0.525δn. A stronger statement about the original selector's private uniform-size moment needs a different construction.

## 1. Two occupied leaves, independent of prime

Identify the cyclic dyadic host group with integers modulo n, where 8 divides n eventually. Select the minimum **numeric label** element of each of the two residue classes modulo two and let a,b be their residues modulo eight. Their parities differ. Define an eligible support S consisting of those two complete classes modulo eight. Retain each eligible point independently with probability q=(21/10)δ and retain every other point with probability zero. This is an auxiliary counting distribution on masks. In the actual proof the source distribution before its existence argument has uniform point probability δ at conductor one. [Original selection inputs](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/topsGroup.lean#L90-L123) · [Canonical orbit representative](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/GlobalTheory/IsResidueClassZero.lean#L264-L275)

Set H to the upper prime scale, x to the family parameter, δ=x^(−ε/8), and h=2+8ε. Here n is bounded above and below by constants times x^h. The following table counts fractions of **all positions** on an occupied orbit. In the second column eligible means membership in S, independent of the random retained trials. The third column counts eligible sites at even orbit positions.

| Divisibility of step d | Eligible fraction f | Eligible even fraction f₀ |
|---|---:|---:|
| d odd | 1/4 | 1/8 |
| d divisible by 2 exactly | 1/4 | 1/4 |
| d divisible by 4 exactly | 1/2 | 1/2 |
| d divisible by 8 | 1 | 1/2 |

The final row assumes even orbit length, as in every orbit reaching the source order cutoff at late x. At valuation one the orbit is an entire parity class. The least label in it lies in the supported leaf and sets the origin. At valuation two an occupied orbit is a smaller class containing that same least-label element, which remains least there. Thus the eligible terms in those two rows are all even. At higher valuations the orbit lies entirely in one leaf. At odd steps the whole group is one orbit and exactly one supported leaf lies on its even half. The pattern of eligibility and parity is periodic with period at most eight.

For odd d not congruent to either ±(a−b) modulo eight, S and S+d are disjoint. Two of the four odd residues have this property. These directions constitute a quarter of **all** directions, uniformly in the field's labeling. No retained pair in either orientation can be adjacent at any of them, even on an orbit of full order n. This claim holds simultaneously for every realization of the auxiliary product measure.

## 2. The original good-orbit budget at its fixed constants

The source condition is tested against the actual pairs of primes and steps on this host. It uses the original short-window occupancy test, the marked-tile test and the larger-window occupancy test. Failure is weighted by the **retained points on a bad orbit**, hence an entirely empty orbit contributes zero. The source bounds the short window length L₂ by a constant depending on the fixed parameters. Denote the cycle length by L, and restrict to the source-tested L≥x^(ε/4). Let K=ceil(8(h+2)/ε), r=K+1, u=ceil(1/δ), M₀=ceil((h+3) log x ·10^(6r+1)) and G′=(M₀+5)(6r+1)u. All constants in the estimates below are uniform over primes, directions, numeric labels, tagged points and eligible cosets. [Three original tests](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SubspaceCounting/GoodOrbitIntervalTileCondition.lean#L9-L26) · [Short-window definition](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SubspaceCounting/StatementB.lean#L9-L14) · [Parameter formulas](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GrowthBounds/UnramifiedQuadraticSetting.lean#L235-L252) · [Short-window length bound](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/topsBase.lean#L42-L69)

**Short windows.** Condition on retention of a given eligible point. At most L₂ starting points produce a window containing the tag, eventually L>L₂. Each of these needs K other successes to violate a cap K. All other windows need K+1. Union bounds over windows and subsets inside each window give
```math
 L_2 {L_2\choose K}q^K + L {L_2\choose K+1}q^{K+1}=o(x^{-2}).
```
Indeed δ^K≤x^(−h−2), q/δ=2.1 is fixed and L=O(x^h). Treating every window as tagged would lose the latter vanishing factor. This detail allows any fixed positive source constant rather than a larger replacement.

**Marked tiles.** Each full cell of width u has fu+O(1) eligible sites, of which f₀u+O(1) are even, independently of its starting phase. Its empty probability tends uniformly to exp(−2.1f). The probability that it has precisely one retained site and that this site is even tends to 2.1f₀ exp(−2.1f). Both limits exceed 1/10 in every row of the table. The smallest empty limit is exp(−2.1)>0.1224 and the smallest singleton-even limit is 1.05 exp(−2.1)>0.1285. Thus every cell has the required type probability ≥1/10 eventually. The estimates concern the cell prescribed to be empty or to have one even success. There is no requirement that every cell be marked.

The (6r+1) cells in a tile are disjoint. Its actual marked-pattern probability is at least 10^(−6r−1). In a prescribed run of M₀+1 distinct complete tiles the fixed tag affects at most one. The remaining tiles are independent. There are at least M₀+1 full tiles available because (M₀+1)(6r+1)u=O(x^(ε/8)log x)=o(x^(ε/4)). Failure for any tile start has conditional probability at most L exp(−M₀10^(−6r−1))≤Lx^(−h−3)=O(x^−3). The wrapping is at the level of complete tiles, as in the source. [Source tile threshold and existence of enough complete tiles](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/orbitTerms.lean#L97-L128)

**Larger windows.** Write k=N₁−1 for the source threshold before the final additive one. It is at least both 6eG′δ and (h+3)log₂x. Even after conditioning on the tag, violation requires at least k successes among the other eligible sites in that window. Their count is dominated by a binomial with G′ trials at rate q. Selecting a k-subset gives probability at most (eG′q/k)^k≤(0.35)^k≤x^(−h−3). If k>G′ the event is empty. Summing over L starts yields O(x^−3). This uses the source's upper occupancy cap at its actual value. [Original occupancy threshold](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/orbitTerms.lean#L225-L250)

**One mask for all primes.** Let W be the sum of the source weights log(p)/p over its tested primes. Conditioning on a tagged retained point gives the preceding three bounds for its fixed orbit. Thus the expected weighted count of retained points on bad orbits is at most o(x^−2)·q|S|·W=o(x^−2δnW). For any fixed positive source Cb, Markov's inequality ensures its original budget with probability tending to one. If W=0 that budget is automatic. This union of estimates does not condition on all directions passing simultaneously without any exception. The actual budget permits a measured total of exceptions. [Pinned BadOK](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/ThinScalesIffHostBound.lean#L35-L42) · [What the host selector returns](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/topsChoiceH.lean#L30-L38)

Each class has expected retained count 21δn/80, and independent Bernoulli variance at most that mean. Chebyshev's inequality gives both counts 21δn/80+o(δn) with probability tending to one, for example with tolerance (δn)^(2/3). This meets the original size bracket [δn/2,2δn] eventually and makes the odd fraction on the missing generator directions 1/2+o(1). At conductor one the share is an identity. Intersect the finitely described conditions above to obtain *one* mask on each original raw host. Its membership subsequently remains fixed at every prime. Notice the distinction between satisfying the exported size bracket and satisfying the tighter private original uniform selector size estimate. The latter requires |N−δn|≤sqrt(2δn), which this first construction fails.

## 3. Size of the recovery on those directions

On each deficient generator direction there is one orbit of length n. At every active interior prime with this step and on any lift j, no first exponent leads from one top to another. When p³≥2H no higher excluded positive contribution survives either, by the cubic threshold on the selected height window. The remaining credit C consists of even-source minima. Its first exponents land on thin partners. Its odd tail is bounded by a single geometric tail. Consequently C/Nλ=o(1) uniformly there. Indeed each first term is at most w″+η+c_G, and each tail is at most 4a/p³. The selected scales satisfy w″/λ=1/n, (c_G+η)/λ=o(1), and xH/p³=o(1) on the interior. By the source price bound and the nonnegative fixed price, Pr≥O(λ−κ_j) where O in this inequality denotes the **number of odd tops**. The normalized excess of direct gain over A=g_U−Pr is at least O/N−o(1) as a fraction of Nλ, because g₀−A=Pr−C. Taking the positive part or allowing the zero floor can only increase this recovery. For these admissible masks the half exposure supplies a lower limit 1/2 per retained-top unit on the deficient directions. [Verified local branch interface](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/3dedd2d81a54d046e3c12ef78df9d9fd0c2ad6dc/research/uriel/experiments/actual-gain-study/evidence/local/SOURCE.md)

The previous same-host pointwise prime-step estimate gives one quarter of generator-and-nongenerator step mass to these residue classes, with the two-sign inert class carrying 1/4 of all prime log-weight. The selected drop and missed-top bounds remove o(HN) weighted top mass. In particular, failure to serve this sole generator orbit would omit *all* N tops at that prime. Removing the end strips from the high band costs o(H). Multiplying the factors gives
```math
 \liminf \mathcal R_x \ \ge\
  2\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac12=\frac1{16}.
```
Here $`\mathcal R_x`$ is the interior fallback recovery in [the prior identity for the actual signed gap](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/3dedd2d81a54d046e3c12ef78df9d9fd0c2ad6dc/research/uriel/experiments/actual-gain-study/evidence/transport/TRANSPORT.md). The prime and drop transports use [the previous prime-distribution and selection argument](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/pull/10). This is a lower bound within the exported contract. No mask of the original family was extracted by this experiment.

## 4. Finite controls and a failed strengthening

All 40,320 numeric label orders on eight positions passed the occupancy and adjacency tests on 403,200 occupied orbits. Choosing the maximum label within each root child as an ablation caused at least one canonical even-coverage failure in every labeling. Another 48 shuffled higher-order labelings added 4,776 occupied orbits. Exact cell-probability checks at widths 7, 8 and 9 failed at least one 1/10 threshold, as finite thresholds can. Every tested width from 15 upward passed. One fixed seeded mask of 131 points on 4096 had 67 odd points, 1024 forced generator directions with zero oriented adjacencies, and no accidentally edge-free additional generator directions. Its normalized absent-step odd mass was 67/1048≈0.06393. This last number uses equal step weights. It is an experiment on a finite cyclic group rather than a census of actual primes. [Pre-run design](DESIGN.md) · [Raw result](results.json) · [Experiment](experiment.py)

To test whether this particular global support mechanism could reach the tighter original size target, the separate [registered modulus-sixteen search](STRONGER_DESIGN.md) tried seven leaves at rate (16/7)δ, at least two leaves on either side of the root, at least one absent generator direction, and valid empty/sole-even probability margins on every occupied dyadic node. A bound on the exponential by its rational Taylor polynomial plus a geometric remainder certifies all accepted margins. Among all 32,768 orientations of the complete depth-four binary tree, 13,824 were covered by the test. The other 18,944 were not certified by this specification. Its first uncovered orientation has bitmask 64, where orientations are ordered root-first, then by increasing residue. This failed strengthening is specific to the seven-leaf layout and its per-cell threshold. It rules out inference of a label-uniform proof from one convenient natural ordering.
