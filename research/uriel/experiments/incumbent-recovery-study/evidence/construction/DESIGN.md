# Registered tests for two occupied classes modulo eight

Before executing the finite tests, fix the two depth-three residue classes containing the least numeric label in each of the two root-parity classes. These are support restrictions used to test necessity of the published output conditions. They do not specify the originally selected set. Fix the inclusion rate at 21δ/10 on this support and zero elsewhere.

1. Enumerate all 8! label orders of the cyclic group of order eight. In each test traverse every nonzero direction and its occupied orbits from the least label. Predict exactly two of the four generator directions have no adjacency between eligible sites in either orientation. For every occupied orbit test the eligible total and even densities against the source-based table. Repeat using shuffled labels and larger dyadic orders to expose assumptions specific to order eight.
2. At cell widths near powers of two and up to 256, calculate conditional empty and sole-even selection probabilities directly from counts for every phase of the periodic supports. Compare with 1/10. The asymptotic proof predicts eventual clearance, but finite widths can fail. Use independent inclusion on occupied sites. Test the negative control obtained by picking the maximum label leaf in each root child, with no requirement that support follows the canonical even child below depth one.
3. At fixed dyadic order 4096 select a single seeded subset, using the prescribed support, then traverse it unchanged over all generator steps. Report the count of zero-adjacency generator steps, point counts, and actual oriented odd fraction. This is a finite structural control. It certifies neither a selected arithmetic witness nor the very large-scale thresholds in the proof.
