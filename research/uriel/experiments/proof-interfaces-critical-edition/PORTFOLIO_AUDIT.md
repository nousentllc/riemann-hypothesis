# Which recent papers needed a new edition?

The audit covers the 16 PDF editions in the local proof sequence from rank-one growth through fixed-datum recovery, 24–26 November 2026. Editions count as separate artifacts. I compared manuscript text, section structure, evidence boundaries and successor results. The hosted-model and circuit experiments belong to a separate series. This is an editorial and mathematical coverage audit, with fresh formal work on the two selected papers. The other papers were read for these decisions, without rerunning their entire studies.

**Decision.** Revise *Where the Two Proof Interfaces Meet* and *From Arithmetic Windows to a Prime Remainder* in one critical edition. Add brief successor links to three older experiment landing pages. Keep the two historical papers, all other PDFs and their release ZIPs available at their original revisions.

## What would merit another substantive revision?

The first genuinely open architecture issue is the transport of selected places and their distribution on a compatible host. A source proof that controls those fibers would change the witness paper. On the local side, identifying all regular tests under an explicit cross-edition transport would change the density comparison. The existing obstruction to the integral trace-one axiom remains in force. Another finite census with the same normalization would not settle either question.
