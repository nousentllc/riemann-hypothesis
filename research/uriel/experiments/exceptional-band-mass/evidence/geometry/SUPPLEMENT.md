# Gap extraction after the primary geometric tests
After deriving the four main formulas and inspecting the primary rational results, examine whether a partly covered prescribed window still contains a smaller fixed-power clean window. If an exceptional set consists of at most k intervals of total log length m in [U,L*U], predict that the largest clean gap has endpoint ratio q satisfying q^(k+1) >= L/(1+m/U). On every mask on a ten-cell rational grid, enumerate connected bad components and measure the best clean gap independently. Include empty/full cases. Also construct a growing number of short interruptions with vanishing total length to test the need for the component hypothesis in the general measurable-interval argument. These generic bad intervals have unrestricted widths.
