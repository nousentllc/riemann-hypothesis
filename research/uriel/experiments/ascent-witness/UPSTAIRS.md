# The unramified extension obtained from an upstairs class group

Let $E$ be a number field and $H$ its Hilbert class field inside an algebraically closed field containing $E$. The original project constructs $H$ as the ray class field of the trivial modulus. It proves finite abelian Galoisness and unramifiedness at all finite and infinite places. Its Artin isomorphism identifies the ideal class group of $E$ with $\mathrm{Gal}(H/E)$. Thus the degree of this particular extension is exactly $h(E)$. [Field, ramification and Artin isomorphism](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/GroundTheory/GlobalFields/ClassFieldConsequences/HilbertClassField.lean#L16-L72)

Put $A=\mathrm{Gal}(H/E)$. Take $J$ to be the subfield of $H$ fixed by the subgroup of cubes $A^3$. This design is canonical once the ambient field is fixed. An arbitrary choice of an index-three subgroup could be exchanged with another one by a downstairs automorphism. Every automorphism of $A$ preserves its cube subgroup.

- If 3 divides $|A|$, Cauchy's theorem supplies a nonidentity element of order 3. Cubing therefore has a nontrivial kernel. On a finite abelian group a surjective endomorphism would be injective, so $A^3$ is proper and $[J:E]>1$.
- Galois correspondence identifies $\mathrm{Gal}(J/E)$ with $A/A^3$. Every element of this quotient cubes to the identity, so this finite group has 3-power order. Its degree can exceed 3.
- Unramifiedness descends from $H/E$ to $J/E$. At a finite prime the proof lifts a prime ideal of $J$ to $H$ and applies the tower criterion. At an infinite place the proof lifts the infinite place and checks that a real place stays real. [Ramification in towers](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/GroundTheory/GlobalFields/ClassFieldConsequences/TrivialModulus.lean#L93-L114)

These conclusions are proved in `UpstairsExtension.lean` for every number field $E$, with no all-layer class-number assumption. The hypothesis $3\mid h(E)$ occurs in the strict positive-degree conclusion. This is the upstairs extension required by a class-field proof by contradiction. The lower-field ramification and normality conditions occur at separate steps. [Lean construction](UpstairsExtension.lean)

The local theorem `elementary_galois_downstairs` closes one further normality question. Suppose the lower field is $F$, with both $E/F$ and $H/F$ Galois. Conjugation by an $F$-automorphism of $H$ takes an $E$-automorphism to another $E$-automorphism. Therefore it carries cubes to cubes and preserves the subfield they fix. The theorem returns $J/F$ Galois. With degree $[E:F]=3$, the separately proved `galois_p_tower` then makes the full Galois group over $F$ a 3-group. Establishing $H/F$ Galois for the concrete tower is a distinct premise of this local theorem.

The completed tower proof supplies that premise. Every $F$-automorphism of $E$ lifts to the ambient field. For this totally complex $E$, transporting the unramified abelian extension along the lift and using the defining maximality of the Hilbert field shows that the lift preserves $H$. The ascent theorem then applies `elementary_galois_downstairs` to the same choice of $H$ and $J$.
