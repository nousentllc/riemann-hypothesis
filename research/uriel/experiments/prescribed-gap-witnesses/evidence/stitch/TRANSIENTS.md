# Follow-up on the sharp crossover

Written after the baseline enumerated two-witness run and the derivation of a proposed refinement, before running this additional control. Test whether the condition needed for *all subsets of a specified label universe* to have oriented endpoint failures is exactly the absence of a label with activation strictly after a, clearance at or before b and a nonempty failure band. Work at exponents Ct/eta=2, 3/2 and 4/3, with integer Ct and eta, rational endpoints including boundaries, and up to 128 labels. Use the power coordinate z=x^(Ct*eta), in which each activation/clearance is an integer. Count windows excluded by the strict crossover but accepted by this test; counts have no arithmetic frequency interpretation. Check the result at the level of singletons, which witness necessity, and exhaustive subsets on a small 8-label subuniverse to check the union argument. Include a concrete nonempty label near onset where powers are too close to produce an obstruction. No original exceptional label is numerically identified.
