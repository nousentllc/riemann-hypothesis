# Can zero contribution genuinely reduce the outer search exponent?

After identifying the direct vanishing route and before building this extension, test two separate claims. With supplied constants unchanged, the two odd length sums may be discharged, but their old ranks can still appear in J. If we construct the global coherent supply afresh and select the *least* admissible odd rank, can the class-ascent proof exhibit ABound at rank zero for both actual fixed fields? Test whether both ranks then become zero in the same coherent supply, so the principal-character exponent depends only on the moving rank. This is a new choice of constants, not an identification of those returned by an old existential call. Attempt a scoped kernel proof and derive the change to the allowed query envelope, without assuming the moving rank or a numerical late threshold.
