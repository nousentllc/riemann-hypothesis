# Forced readings and surviving switch settings

Prospective design, 24 November 2026. In the preceding threshold experiment the only two sessions that selected decisive measurements both returned defective certificates. Those observations motivate this study; they provide no randomized estimate of what happens after a decisive reading. Here the experiment supplies the reading itself.

## Question and matched presentation

Use six newly constructed synthetic DC layouts disjoint from all fourteen designs in the two preceding studies. Each circuit has three binary switches and eight named settings with signed rational currents. For each layout select an initial lamp observation that admits four states, two on each side of the strict current threshold. Supply both possible outcomes of one decisive meter in separate records. Its high and low outcomes each leave two states of a common sign, and the signs reverse between outcomes. Include a third record giving a reading of another physically defined meter that leaves two states of opposite signs. All 18 records therefore have two surviving IDs. Require three layouts in which a high decisive meter indicates yes and three in which it indicates no. Verify all chosen intersections, truth labels, current calculations and the topology separation independently. Do not provide a designated actual switch setting. The question concerns all settings consistent with the two observations.

For each record run the same local agent twice in fresh contexts. The full view displays eight rows, including six excluded by the observations. The filtered view removes exactly those six lines and preserves the order and all fields in the remaining two. All text outside those rows, including both observations, is identical. The agent sees the target truth column and the public numerical inputs in both views. It is told that every compatible setting is included. Each answer schema permits all eight candidate IDs, in the same order across paired views, so the output grammar itself cannot choose the survivors. Neither view makes a lookup. The content of the full record mathematically determines its filtered counterpart. The filtered display performs a calculation on the agent's behalf and reduces the prompt length. The treatment tests that combined effect.

Lock all prompts, branches, keys, settings, schedule and scoring rules before the first inference. Keep the same gpt-oss:20b MXFP4 digest, low reasoning, temperature zero, 8192 context, maximum 2048 generated tokens per session, same 125-word system instruction as the predecessor and the amended server pin Ollama 0.40.18. Within each record use the same seed. Balance full-first and filtered-first orders across high and low decisive records and controls. Randomize sequence in two waves with seed 2026112443. Each record appears once in each wave. Thirty-six single request sessions cost $0 in paid API charges; runtime and electricity are not estimated in dollars. Record request hashes and token counts, and log visible answers. No resampling invalid model decisions. An unacknowledged interruption is counted as missing.

## Claims and endpoints

Primary endpoint: a topology succeeds if the arm certifies BOTH decisive records. For a definitive answer the verdict must be warranted and the submitted ID list must equal the surviving set exactly, with no duplicates. For an ambiguous control both IDs must be compatible and of opposite signs. Each truth class contains one surviving ID on every control in this study, so the two required ID sets have identical size in every stratum. A missing or truncated completion is retained with zero credit. Use the six topology pairs as the independent units of comparison in the design. Report the two off-diagonal counts, both, neither, two-sided exact conditional sign test and the exact conditional 95% interval on the direction of discordance. With no discordance the interval is undefined. This is an intentionally constructed small diagnostic, without a target-population frequency.

Separate the observed sign from the independently scored ID set in every response, including when one is warranted and the other fails. For the twelve determinate records report exact ID sets, warranted signs, unsupported definitive claims, unnecessary abstentions, omitted or extra IDs and verdict reversals across twins. Report the same sign, ID, and certification counts on the six genuinely ambiguous controls. Report malformed decisions, length limits, and model service and token costs. Label all first-failure summaries descriptive. Always give complete case-level evidence for a reader to reconstruct every result.

H1. Filtering improves certified updates on both outcomes of a layout. For resource allocation, prioritize a fresh study of how agents eliminate candidates if the filtered arm gains at least two net complete decisive families, gains at least two net exact ID sets over the twelve decisive cases, and certifies at least as many ambiguous controls. H2. The filtered arm can copy the two visible IDs but still fails to give the warranted sign, especially on ambiguous mixed pairs. Compare sign and ID gains independently. H3. Both views may reach a ceiling, both may fail, or gains may be mixed. Preserve all outcomes, compare simple copying and single-lamp policies, and distinguish a handling limitation from a genuine lack of information. These exploratory gates do not establish independent replication or a change in model weights. The pilot does not assess physical circuit solving, measurement selection or open-ended research.

A filtered session can succeed by copying both visible IDs and checking unanimity in their truth labels. That observation is part of the interpretation. A positive result would demonstrate a benefit of removing excluded rows from this display. It could also reflect shorter inputs or fewer distracting labels. A design separating those explanations would need another matched presentation.
