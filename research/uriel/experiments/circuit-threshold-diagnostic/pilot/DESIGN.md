# Does a displayed comparison help the model choose a decisive meter?

Prospective within-record development diagnostic, 24 November 2026. The preceding frozen study supplied every comparator value but required comparing exact signed currents with a threshold. Its procedure arm made no productive circuit lookup in four opportunities. We designed this experiment to separate the display of that comparison from the physical information available.

## Intervention

Freeze six new circuit topologies, each with an actionable record and a second record obtained by changing the initial observation. Three second records are settled and three remain ambiguous after any one menu item. An actionable record starts with both claim signs possible, and exactly one meter settles the claim for every admissible reading. On the designated actual state each other meter leaves both signs possible. Demand three positive and three negative actionable targets, and two decisive meters in each of the three menu positions. Verify fresh circuits against the eight earlier layouts. Use two independent exact physics calculations before inference.

Give the same local gpt-oss:20b instance both views in isolated sessions. Keep the original proof-derived 125-word system instruction, public candidate universe, named circuit, exact current, initial reading, menu, output schema, inference parameters and lookup budget. In the labeled view append only the header target_current_above_threshold and Y or N per candidate row. The actual state and the admissible set are never marked. The added bit is a deterministic function of two already public numbers. No changes to ordering, grammar or other cues are permitted between these two full user passages. Labels increase token count and change attention cues; they need not isolate arithmetic computation.

Pair the random seed by record. Counterbalance which view is served first over topologies on the actionable records; use the opposite assignment for their control twins. Shuffle the 12 records in each of two waves with seed 2026112429, so both occurrences of a record begin fresh and are separated by a wave boundary. No tools beyond one deterministic menu lookup. Pin model digest, Ollama version 0.40.4, low reasoning, temperature zero, 8192 context, 2048 completion cap per call. Freeze prompts, keys, order, protocol, runner and scorer in Git before sending the first scored prompt. Freshness describes our records and sessions. It makes no claim about model pretraining.

## Endpoints and alternatives

The primary outcome is the choice of the uniquely decisive meter on the six actionable records. A missing response or NONE earns zero. Report labeled-only wins, raw-only wins, both and neither, exact two-sided conditional sign test, and the conditional 95% binomial interval on discordant pairs. There is one primary observation per topology. Six deliberately selected circuits do not provide population prevalence. Key secondary endpoint: certified final answer on those same six. A determinate verdict needs exactly every surviving candidate ID and the warranted sign. An underdetermined verdict requires two genuinely admissible opposite witnesses. Allow no credit for stopping short of a one-lookup answer even if locally honest.

Across all 12 records per arm, score initial signs and certificates, unsupported assertions over actual turns, requests including unnecessary requests on settled cases, final sign and certificate, as well as both-record family success. Report settled and irreducibly ambiguous controls separately. On an actionable record classify failure first by selection (none/missing, wrong meter), then final decision/missing output, then certificate. Conditional post-lookup scores describe the observed path, not an independently randomized treatment. Report length-limited and malformed turns plus token and service costs. Preserve every visible completion, exact request hash and actual meter reply. Two paired records from one topology are not independent demonstrations of transfer.

H1. More visible truth labels improve meter selection. Treat a net gain of at least two decisive selections plus at least one net gain in certified actionable answers as a reason to design a new independently keyed representation study. H2. Labeling improves initial or final certificates, but leaves action selection flat; the data would support a narrower response-format or evidence-reading benefit. H3. Even with truth labels the model frequently omits the decisive observation. Persistent failures would motivate a separate forced-observation or explicit-partition control. These are development interpretations, not significance thresholds. Opposite-direction changes also count and are reported. The existing retrieval replication remains governed by the old decision rule.

Use only the local loopback server with zero paid API spend. Run 24 sessions with at most 48 calls total and no added retries of model decisions. An interrupted in-flight call is missing; a session may resume from a saved acknowledged reply under the same hashes. Abort on model/server/hash mismatch. Preserve controls, signs and thresholds after freezing. Release raw visible outputs, scoring and independent checks, PDF, editable single-source manuscript and ZIP.
