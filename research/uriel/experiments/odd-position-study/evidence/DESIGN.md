# Experiments on the odd-position allowance

This pass starts from the [served-prime experiment](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/pull/9) and the original source at 40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005. The mathematical candidate explanations were developed during source reading. The following protocol precedes running the dyadic enumeration. It is a local pre-execution record, published together with its output.

1. For a cyclic dyadic group, permute the numeric labels independently of the cyclic group coordinates. Enumerate all nonzero steps and all points, relative to the source's least base in each orbit. Predict that exposure to odd positions over uniform steps (zero counted as even) permutes the scores 0/n through (n−1)/n, and is constant by generator choice inside each valuation layer. Compare a direct orbit traversal to the binary partition argument. Include deliberately scrambled labels and a failure of the assumption that raw label parity always identifies odd positions.
2. Enumerate all fixed-size retained sets in a 16-point group. Compare the greatest mean exposure with the greedy bound after imposing a per-cell occupancy cap at a specified depth. Record an attaining subset, its signed-rank score, and the unconstrained maximum. Vary the density cap and labels. The cap is a relaxation of linked original conditions. A finite maximizer is evidence of sharpness only in that relaxation.
3. Check the uniform-step identity for adjacent retained pairs by direct counting, including the identity step. Test extreme subsets. This is a finite check of the group argument used after prime equidistribution. It is independent of conjectural prime samples.
4. At fixed scales compare a signed per-position charge and its full unused-partner correction with the exact nearest-even distance. Distinguish complete clipping, partial clipping and an odd position without an adjacent even predecessor. Include an absent even-top control and distinguish it from a selected good orbit. Check the source stages needed to carry a credit without changing the host.
5. For the source's allowed interval of independent inclusion probabilities, evaluate the maximum and minimum ratio of expected odd exposure to expected retained size. Provide the individual-witness concentration argument separately. Uniform choices, root parity bias, and threshold bias are separate interventions. Additional selection guarantees refer to a strengthened existential construction.

The fixed-witness upper bound, estimates for alternative witnesses, local signed charge, positive part of that charge and actual demand-minus-gain gap are distinct claims. Test results are not evidence for equality in the final global inequality.

After the dyadic enumeration and before the arithmetic run, specify a separate prime control. Take the real 256th cyclotomic factor (64 step labels) and the two independent quadratic signs of discriminants −163 and −25799. At strict cutoffs 10,000, 100,000 and one million, count inert primes by their dyadic step, computed as the unique discrete logarithm to base 5 up to sign mod 256. Compare odd and unpaired retained fractions for four fixed subsets against exact uniform-step predictions. The prime table measures an explicit abelian model. It carries no selected-family certificate.
