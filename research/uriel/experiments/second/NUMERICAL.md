# What the finite experiments found

The two protocols were recorded before their respective outputs. We subsequently added an exact finite-window enclosure and three scalar budget perturbations plus an amplified-product check. All six raw result files reproduced byte for byte under Python 3.13.2 and 3.12.9 with mpmath 1.3.0. Each run passed 26 regression tests. Rerunning the first suite with its pinned NumPy and SciPy also reproduced its numerical data and lexical graph byte for byte and passed 25 tests. A preliminary first-suite launch under a different SciPy installation failed to import its native sparse solver. The successful replay used the first suite's specified versions. ## 1. Resolving two nearby nonreal poles

On the real axis a conjugate pair at equal distances can cancel. For the registered pair at $3/4\pm(9/4)i$, the relative signed coefficient was below $2\times10^{-112}$ at all five designated orders in the 110-digit run. At the vertically aligned center the triangle bound first gave 1% relative error at order 5. The source uses a shifted center for nonreal poles and requires a positive residue on a real pole. A negative real exponential has zero positive envelope throughout and violates that last hypothesis. [Pole assumptions](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauZeroFreeCriterion.lean#L15-L24) · [Source case split](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindow.lean#L83-L160) · [First-round residue and delayed-window controls](../initial/INTERPRETATION.md)

## 2. The one-sided estimate and the actual prime sum

With $g(u)=e^{3u/4}\cos(\omega u)$, all 36 full-window comparisons had nonnegative margin after multiplying by $(\sigma-\theta)^k$. At $\sigma=336$, the smallest margin was $2.0333\times10^{-8}$, for $U=2,\ k=840,\ \omega=1,\ \gamma=0$. Across all central outputs the two precisions agreed to $9.8\times10^{-67}$ in units of $\max(1,|a|,|b|)$. Integrating the complex moments directly at orders 420 and 840 gave discrepancies below $1.3\times10^{-111}$ at 110 digits. The estimated omitted integrals are below $5\times10^{-299}$ in both cases. The negative exponential control has zero combined left side. Removing its real derivative term made all four comparisons fail, with scaled margins from −0.00407 to −0.03651. The derivative of $G(y)/\sqrt y$ has the sign of $A-2-\log y+B/y$, strictly decreasing on each smooth piece. Evaluating the endpoints, kinks and every interior stationary candidate gives a continuum search on each specified interval. Logarithms and bisection were numerical. ## 3. Integer feasibility at the arithmetic interface

All 36 grid entries satisfied the scalar `Scale`, `Ranks`, all four `RankVal` clauses, the cyclic exponent lower bound, and the modeled `HostVal` cardinality bounds. The source's strict epsilon cutoff is $1/52$. The frame order is $n=2^e$, $f=2n$, $r=k+m-1$. We used the source's even ceiling for $m,k$ and reconstructed the odd $t$ from $r+t=8fr+4+\mathrm{TOv}\,\mathrm{nWv}$. The largest $t$ needed 1,518,074 bits. Its value is reproducible from the row parameters and a big-endian SHA-256 fingerprint. Increasing $t$ by two preserved every scalar rank inequality and broke the exact identity in all 36 rows. Flooring a fractional density threshold violated its lower bound in all 27 fractional TT cases and all 18 fractional T2 cases. [Rank witness construction](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/ExistsRanksRankValFrame.lean#L45-L121) · [Rank and host conditions](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/PairRowIffQdRows.lean#L16-L85)

Let $L=x^\varepsilon$, $d_T=n_T/n$ and $d_2=n_{T2}/n$. For $a_E/b=x$, division by $n_Tmb d_2/2$ gives

$$
S=\frac{2x}{m d_2}(1+m^2/k)+\frac{4m L}{d_Td_2}+\frac{2kL^2}{d_Tn^2}.

The unnormalized and normalized evaluations agreed exactly in every row. All 24 rows meeting every modeled size condition passed. The other 12 failed only $26^8\le L^3$. Six of these still met the envelope and six exceeded it. Thus failure of the sufficient absorption condition does not determine what happens on an individual finite row. ## 4. How the finite exceptions arise

For integer centers at prime $\ell$, strict proximity $|s-e|_\ell<\ell^{-\lfloor\log_\ell Z\rfloor}$ places $e$ in a residue class modulo $\ell^{\lfloor\log_\ell Z\rfloor+1}>Z$. In the source the center can lie in a normed extension. The proof compares two candidate integers via their difference. Across 40 prime/range pairs, every strict class contained at most one integer in $[1,Z]$. Weakening strict proximity produced collisions in 24 pairs. For $\ell=2,Z=7,s=1$, the strict list is $\{1\}$ and the weak list is $\{1,5\}$. Repeated centers can reduce the cardinality of the union. [Original uniqueness proof](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/Near1.lean#L6-L43) · [Construction of the exceptional set](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/LayeredIsomorphisms/Exc.lean#L7-L30)

The two-primary and odd-prime arguments choose their sets at $Z=\lfloor X^{C_t}\rfloor$. Their bounds are added before choosing one window for the union. Goodness then passes to every subset by restriction of its universal quantifier. For $C_t=2,\eta=1$ we compared integer powers exactly, covering the entire real window. Seven assembled cases passed for the union and each component. The naive choice $Y=\sqrt X$ failed in six cases, including all three moving-exception cases. These tests model the selector and finite near-sets. The existence of the upstream cohomological bounds, extension fields and Sha lengths is additional mathematics. ## 5. Why the scalar slope constant is twelve

Take $d_g=3,d_b=1,\delta=1/12,Q=m=\ell=1,\mu=-1,\mu_g=-2$. The discarded-rank inequality and the weighted cut are equalities. The scalar conclusion $\mu-C\delta Q\le\mu_g$ reduces to $C\ge12$. Even $C=11.999$ fails. For a given positive discarded fraction $\alpha=d_b/(d_g+d_b)$, the cut bounds the loss by $3Q\alpha/(1-\alpha)$. Substituting $\alpha\le3\delta$ explains both the smaller coefficient available away from the boundary and the endpoint value. [Original numeric lemma and geometric application](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/MajAtAddRight.lean#L93-L114) · [New Lean statement](Sharpness.lean)

Eight Euclidean orthogonal projections removed the line generated by $v=(1,q,0,\ldots)$ in dimensions four and eight. Both intersection Gram determinants were $g=1+q^2$, the direct-sum index was $g$, and $g$ was the least common matrix denominator. With squared-covolume slope convention, the good rank-weighted slope is $-\log g$. A zero denominator allowance fails for every $q$ tested even with the discarded-rank condition satisfied. These examples establish denominator sensitivity in an integral Euclidean model. Whether the scalar endpoint tuple occurs for the number-field lattices in the formalization remains open in this investigation. ## What would distinguish the remaining explanations

1. **Analytic.** Extend the finite-window enclosure to a primitive nonprincipal character. Its $L'(1)/L(1)$ term requires an independent normalization and an error bound for the value used.
2. **Arithmetic and cohomology.** Trace one compatible `PairRow` and its exceptional-set bounds into the same `CoreAtH` witness. Separate existential source steps from genuinely computable quantities. This tests what survives the passage from independently feasible numbers to jointly constructed data. [Extraction of the tied family](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimitiveCosets/LevelGmLeOnFamilyScales.lean#L13-L30)
3. **Covolume.** Determine whether the geometric hypotheses bound $m+\ell-\mu$ more tightly than $3Q$ on actual lattices. The scalar witness rules out improving 12 from the displayed scalar hypotheses alone.
