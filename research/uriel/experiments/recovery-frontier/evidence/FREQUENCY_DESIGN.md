# Predeclared frequency tests

The main conjecture for this panel is that the least universal upper bound for the asymptotic fraction of the local odd allowance recovered by the fallback equals one. The critical order of limits is to fix the source epsilon and desired accuracy, construct a finite-dimensional smooth torus profile, and only then let the scale grow. Independent source checks must cover the original selector's score, bad-prime weights, odd/even tile probabilities, and the actual gain sum.

1. For a finite sanity check, use the binary vector groups of dimensions 16, 19 and 22, a single mask with 512 distinct points at each dimension, and seed 7021. Enumerate the support of its nonoriented differences exactly and evaluate all its Fourier coefficients with an integer Walsh transform. Compare with a 512-point subgroup, which leaves almost all differences missing but fails a hyperplane occupancy test. Predict that missing differences exceed 95% at dimension 22, while all random-mask hyperplanes contain points and the Fourier bound still forces positive occupancy on every codimension-two flat. Record failures without adjusting the seed. This is a different finite group from the arithmetic host.
2. Verify the bound for r independent resonances over composite grids by enumeration, including matrices of nonunit determinant as well as a dependent-matrix ablation. Use moduli 8, 16 and 32, three two-row matrices including determinant 1, 2 and 4, radius 1 or 2 grid spacings. The determinant factor and rank assumption are both essential.
3. Certify the extreme-size existence example using integer exponent arithmetic, without generating its centers. Fix epsilon=1/64, resonance rank 4096, box side 1/8 and power-of-two dimensions from 2^16 through 2^23. Bound Fourier truncation, the probability of a translate-bias failure, and the union bound on difference boxes. Report the first dimension in this grid that verifies 99% recovery by the analytic sufficient conditions. Finite original scales, the existential mask, its Lean evaluation and the full RH endpoint are outside this certificate.
