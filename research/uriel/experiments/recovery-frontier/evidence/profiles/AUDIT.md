# A fully enumerated 32-residue construction

For any numeric labeling on the cyclic dyadic conductor-one host, the following fixed-size profile permits at least **10367/124242 = 8.3441992%** recovery of the local odd-position allowance. This is the smallest best ratio over all canonical orderings in this specified construction. The class is narrow enough that its internal upper and lower values match exactly. It supplies a finite alternative to the manuscript's high-dimensional existence construction.

## Support and rates

Choose the least-labeled point as additive origin. Its mod-32 class is the only supported class in its root-even half. Set its inclusion rate to \(q_e\delta\) with \(q_e=119/50\). Each odd residue can be written \(2i+1\) with \(i\in\{0,\ldots,15\}\). Reflection around the chosen origin pairs \(i\) with \(15-i\). In the root-odd half consider a candidate pair of reflected residues to remove. If removing a leaf empties the canonical even child of a surviving parent, remove its sibling too. Continue upward to the smallest complete block whose removal leaves the source's even-child requirement intact. The multiset of these deletion costs over sixteen leaves is eight ones, four twos, two fours, one eight and one sixteen. This follows because the cost doubles once per consecutive canonical-even branch traversed upward from the leaf. At least one of the eight reflected pairs has combined deletion cost at most three. Otherwise every cost-one leaf would be paired with a leaf of cost at least four, and there are only four such leaves. The two leaves of a reflected pair lie in different children of the root-odd half, so their small deletion blocks are disjoint.

Let \(m\in\{13,14\}\) be the odd leaves remaining. Retain a fixed number in each supported class at common rate \(q_o\delta\), where \(q_o=(32-q_e)/m\). Round within a bounded number of points over the 32 strata to give the total \(N\) nearest \(\delta n\). Sampling independently among fixed-sized subsets of the participating classes is a way of proving existence on the actual raw host. The original uniform selector's score is evaluated for the resulting subset. Its size term is \(o(1)\). The rates of all positive classes are smaller than 2.4 times the original rate.

## Literal mark threshold

In each consecutive cell of length \(u=\lceil1/\delta\rceil\), let \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) be the limiting sums of pointwise Bernoulli probabilities over all sites and over even sites. Congruence patterns are periodic with bounded period, so the limits hold uniformly in the starting site. In the separate product law at these marginals, the empty-cell probability tends to \(e^{-\mu}\) and the exactly-one-even probability tends to \(\nu e^{-\mu}\). The source requires \(3r+1\) empty cells and \(3r\) even-singleton cells. Thus the per-cell geometric factor is \(e^{-\mu}\nu^{3r/(6r+1)}\). Its being strictly greater than \(1/10\) suffices. Treating each type separately would lose the present construction at the root. ## Gain transport and the denominator

Exactly two of the 32 step residues are blocked in both orientations on the full generator orbit. They are the missing opposite pair. There are at most three deleted odd leaves. No other opposite pair can be fully removed. The root-odd retained mass tends to \(s=1-q_e/32=1481/1600\) of the total. The normalized recovery from these directions tends to \(\tfrac12(2/32)s\). No other orbit with positive odd exposure lacks both edge orientations. In the odd half, each subtree with at least eight leaves has more than half its leaves supported. For a subtree with four or fewer leaves, any nonempty set meeting both parities allows at least one first edge in each unsigned generator direction. A surviving subtree entirely in one parity is in its canonical even parity. All finer occupied leaves are full. Each allowed orientation occurs at least once per 32 consecutive sites, and each supported rate is a positive fixed multiple of \(\delta\). Conditioning on a bounded number of modal stratum counts costs a polynomial factor. Chernoff bounds therefore give many realized edges of each allowed orientation on every long orbit simultaneously. The high-band penalty and reverse-edge clipping estimates established in the preceding study make all remaining normalized recovery vanish. The means of the sixteen odd leaves, multiplied by 64, are the odd integers from 33 to 63, placed by the actual canonical order. Variance \(O(N)\) of the bounded exposure score permits concentration on the same fixed-size mask as the good-score and matching events. If \(z\) denotes the sum of these integer scores on the \(m\) retained odd leaves, the limiting ratio is
\[
   \frac{(2/32)s}{[(1-s)+s z/m]/64}.
