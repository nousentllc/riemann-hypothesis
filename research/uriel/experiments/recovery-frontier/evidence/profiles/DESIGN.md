# Root directions under the actual score

Predeclare a modulus-32 support. Use the single root-even leaf containing the least numeric label at rate 119/50 times the baseline density. Among the root-odd leaves reflect about that leaf, then remove opposite leaves together with the smallest blocks whose deletion preserves the canonical-even child of every surviving parent. Select a reflected pair with total deletion cost at most three. Fill the remaining odd leaves at a common rate chosen to give the original cardinality. Compare the true product probability of a marked tile with the tenth-to-the-power threshold using rational exponential bounds. Exhaust the 32768 root-odd subtrees of depth four with both natural and canonically flipped orderings of the full group represented after translation. Aggregate all viable supports for each orientation, compute the best lower bound from holes, and report the worst of those best bounds. Check the first-step table and enumerate all possible anchored finite paths on one small transfer example. The search should also detect any failure of canonical occupancy or of the tile condition. Treat any result for a restricted quotient as a restricted result.
