# Registered deterministic upper checks

Work on cyclic quotients of size 4, 8 and 16. A leaf with positive rate represents a whole residue class in a sufficiently long arithmetic host. The rates sum to the quotient size. We fix the event that every geometrically available first-step orientation on a long orbit has enough actual selected neighbors for the specialized branch to win. A zero-support odd child has zero cost and a positive odd child with an empty even child violates the canonical marked-tile condition.

Test the following before deriving numerical envelopes. For nonempty child supports, direct traversal of all odd step generators sees either orientation on at least the larger child's relative support size. Across the complete dyadic tree, predicted survival is at least the reciprocal rate cap times the local allowance. Use each of the 255 nonempty subsets of the order-eight quotient for four distinct label orders, and 64 deterministic nonconstant rate patterns with natural and scrambled labels at sizes 4, 8 and 16. Check the exact marked-child geometry, group sums, deep homogeneous tail and ordering bounds using integer and rational arithmetic. Include a control at modulus two suppressing the only canonical even child, and an explicit example where a forward-only count misses a reverse edge. Independently check whether the two-orientation overlap count satisfies the stronger balanced-children inequality min(m,2 min(e,o)) for every pair of nonempty children at m=1,2,4,8. Report which part uses a finite-period assumption. A search over binary quotient templates cannot certify a strict limiting bound for unrestricted masks.
