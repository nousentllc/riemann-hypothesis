# Scored-form menu amendment before evaluating either arm

A pre-outcome oracle audit of the initial formal design found that always picking a menu item with the most distinct possible readings could reach the target on all six formal families, assuming otherwise perfect classification and certificates. It would not need to ask whether a reading separates *opposite answers*. Before scored dispatch, an independent case author added genuine but less informative observations and rearranged their positions. The proof families, visible base evidence, underlying objects, truths, paired initial screens, target strata, procedure, general checklist, scoring thresholds and development records are preserved. The circuit records are unchanged. The diagnostic folder retains the original results and the corresponding post-amendment analysis. Published rules from the preceding development registrations and their files are preserved so both calibrations can be reproduced. The scored freeze uses register_scored.py; run.py administers both frozen registries identically.
