# Exceptional local orders and a larger class of multipliers

Under the original hypotheses (including Hermitian rank M ≥ 3 and nonzero local fiber measures at every finite place of the fixed target β), Lean proves the following.

There exist a fixed positive integer N and a finite exceptional set B. Give each v in B any nonnegative order cap H(v). There is a constant c_H > 0 which works for every positive integer t satisfying ord_v(t) ≤ H(v) at all v in B. The product of the original fiber measures on the original dilated sets at a = Nt is at least c_H a^([F:Q](2M−2)). Capping order at zero permits all simultaneous units. Allowing positive caps strictly enlarges the possible class in examples. This uses the same full exponent as the original growth theorem.

- [Local floor on the original Gram-family masses](verification/ExceptionalDilation.lean). Inclusion of the undilated lattice in its t-dilate and the exact scaling identity give D_v(t²z) ≥ D_v(z) q_v^(−H(v)(2M−2)). The positive constant attached to the exceptional set is a product of these floors. Neither a numerical value of N nor a specific B follows without fixing an input and its local witnesses.
- [Global bounded-order theorem](verification/CappedGrowth.lean). The constant is chosen before t. The full summable error at all good places controls the moving prime divisors. WithinCap spells out the ideal-power valuation used in the pinned theorem. There is no uniformity claim as the caps vary.
- [Four-square control at every depth](verification/FourSquareControl.lean). For every k ≥ 1 and every depth r ≥ 2k+1 the exact normalized four-square count modulo 2^r at 4^k is 3/4^k. The module also proves directly that every proposed positive floor fails along a suitable target and all its stable depths. These finite representation densities occur in the adjacent Gaussian rank-two model. Their raw scaled values are 3. This establishes the limitation of an argument based solely on raw monotonicity. The rank-two example lies outside the original M ≥ 3 hypotheses. The control does not identify a rank-two original measure family.
- [Independent experiments](exceptional_experiment.py) and [results](exceptional_experiment.json). The executable reproduced 25 four-square target-depth counts (through modulus 2048), 8 six-square comparisons at the sibling targets three and twelve, direct enumeration at moduli 2, 4 and 8, and a full period for B = {2,3} over Q. The two rank-three finite densities are 5/4 and 65/64 at tested depths 4 through 11. Their normalized ratio is 13/16 and their raw rescaled ratio is 13. No all-depth rank-three theorem at these two targets is asserted here. The illustrative set of rational primes was chosen for the period check.
