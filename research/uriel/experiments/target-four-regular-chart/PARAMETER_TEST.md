# Additional readable-coordinate control

After the original six-square counts and the hyperbolic frame were known, we derived a rectangle in the readable parameters. This is a follow-up control, not a prediction registered before those original counts. For a vector box centered at the real pivot a with width w equal to 8 or 16, restrict p to a+wB, the residual v to wB and the skew scalar to (w/2)O. Enumerate one further digit of each of their five scalar coordinates. Map all 1,024 equal-volume parameter cells through the literal rational chart in the frame. Predict 1,024 distinct original vector children, the same children as direct squared-norm evaluation, and no multiplicities. Include all four target classes inside the first parent, two finer parent boxes, and the empty pivot-10 case at four. The test should fail if the free coordinate is erroneously narrowed by another factor of two. The proof of stabilization and the chart/source normalizations are independent obligations.
