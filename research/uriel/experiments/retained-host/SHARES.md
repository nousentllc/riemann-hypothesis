# What one swap changes in a retained set

The order of the quotient decides whether distribution across cosets can distinguish two sets. For the cyclic level generated by the principal conductor-one character, that order is one and every set meets the share condition at any nonnegative tolerance. On a published index-two frame, one exchange between cosets changes the smallest allowed tolerance from zero to one seventh. It also reverses the sign of a character sum used in the next proof step.

## The actual predicate

For a frame φ let A be its indexed finite abelian group, A₀ the subgroup generated by its distinguished element, and o the index of A₀. For a retained set T of size m let nγ be the number of its elements in γ + A₀. The formal condition is an absolute bound for **every** γ, including γ outside T,

    |nγ − λγ m| ≤ δ m.

Here λγ is 4/(3o+1) for γ in A₀ and 3/(3o+1) otherwise. The weights sum to one across the o distinct cosets. The source proves the equality between its integer parameter o and the subgroup index. ## One explicit frame and all eight sets

Take the source constructor `cyclicFramePow 3 1`. Its coordinate group is ℤ/8ℤ, its generator is 2, and its distinguished subgroup is {0,2,4,6}. The two weights are therefore 4/7 and 3/7. The coordinate isomorphism lets us specify the actual elements of each Lean finset by their residues. Its construction uses the source's chosen cyclic isomorphism to the cyclotomic quotient. The experiment computes within ℤ/8ℤ and proves the transport to the literal source frame. [Frame construction and index](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/FrameCyclicGenerator.lean#L112-L174)

Let Tᵢ contain all residues except i. At m=7 the target counts are 4 and 3.

| Omitted residue | Even count | Odd count | Minimum δ | Sum of the parity character |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7, reference | 4 | 3 | 0 | +1 |
| 5, swap within the odd coset | 4 | 3 | 0 | +1 |
| 6, swap between cosets | 3 | 4 | 1/7 | −1 |

The parity character is +1 on the even coset and −1 on the odd coset. Its sum is the difference of the two counts. The source's `tops_sum` predicts a main term m/(3o+1)=1 for a nontrivial character trivial on A₀, with error at most oδm=14δ. The cross-coset swap yields absolute error 2. It violates the proposed bound at δ=1/8, where the allowance is 7/4, and attains equality at δ=1/7. The premise fails precisely below that boundary. This identifies a concrete failure of the intermediate character estimate when one ignores that premise. The prime statistic in the later inequality also depends on additional arithmetic data. [Character estimate](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimitiveCosets/TopsSum.lean#L75-L100)

Independent exact-rational enumeration gives four passing subsets out of eight at both δ=0 and δ=1/8, and eight at δ=1/7. Among all 40,320 permutations of the labels, 576 preserve both cosets, 576 interchange them, and the remaining 39,168 mix the cosets. At δ=0 the first 576 preserve every subset's outcome and the second 576 reverse every outcome. Tests enumerate all eight subsets for every permutation. These permutations relabel retained elements with the distinguished subgroup held fixed. Many are permutations of a set rather than group automorphisms. ## What happens at conductor one

For the cyclic level generated by `PChar.one`, the subgroup of character powers is trivial. The field `EAtH.o_eq` forces the frame index to equal that level's index, one. The identity `A₀.index = o` then gives A₀=A. Every nγ equals m, every λγ equals 1, and the left-hand error is zero even for an empty T. This conclusion applies whenever the level is exactly `Level.cyc PChar.one` or the frame is otherwise established to have index one. Merely including the principal character among the kept characters does not determine the index. In particular the finding by itself does not determine a center, an exceptional integer, or a witness for a specific `PairRow`. [Level definitions](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L105-L127)

## Where the original retained set comes from

The source assigns probability d to elements of A₀ and 3d/4 to elements outside it, with d=x^(−ε/8). Its finite moment argument bounds a combined expression involving squared deviations of coset counts and a weighted sum of bad events. It then obtains a single T meeting both constraints. The conversion to the share inequality preserves that T. `topsFrame` transfers it to the frame, and `topsBase` asserts density, share, and weighted orbit bounds together for the updated arithmetic base. In particular, computing a compatible coset histogram is only one of the replayable pieces of the host construction. [Finite selection](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/select.lean#L70-L82) · [Count-to-share transfer](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/topsGroup.lean#L90-L142) · [Retained base](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/topsBase.lean#L79-L92)

The finite example supplies the published Frame interface, its subgroup and projected generator, and an explicit retained set. It supplies no number-field host, selected family index, bounded-character estimates or certification of the required weighted bad-orbit sum. The original `topsBase` also requires sufficient scale and x^(2+8ε)/2 ≤ n with 0<ε<1/52. Already at x≥16 that lower bound exceeds the order eight of this frame. Thus 1/8 and 1/7 in the control are test tolerances of the predicate. An attempted identification with an asymptotic proof scale would fail the frame-size condition. A direct next test is to request a nontrivial quotient character on an explicitly identified index-greater-than-one family, retaining its level and scale through the same transfer.
