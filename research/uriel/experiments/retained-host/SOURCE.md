## How the proof picks one set for two jobs

Let $N=|A|$, and let $o$ be the index of the distinguished subgroup $A_0$. At scale $x$ the construction uses inclusion weight $d=x^{-\varepsilon/8}$ inside $A_0$ and $3d/4$ outside. It considers the product weights of all subsets. The score has two contributions, the sum of squared coset-count errors divided by $D^2$, and a normalized, weighted count of bad orbit events. Here $D=\sqrt{2dN}$. A second-moment bound controls the first contribution and an expectation bound controls the second. The finite averaging lemma selects a positive-weight set with score at most the mean. The zero-mean bad-cost case has its own argument. This is the point at which the proof obtains a witness without giving a subset of a particular numerical frame. [Finite averaging and selection](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/select.lean#L70-L164)

The expected count inside one distinguished coset is $d|A_0|$ and in each other coset is $3d|A_0|/4$. Normalizing across the $o$ cosets explains the weights $4/(3o+1)$ and $3/(3o+1)$. The conversion theorem accounts for the fluctuation of the *realized total* $|T|$. It returns a cardinality interval and a discrepancy bound relative to that total. In `topsGroup`, the tolerance passed to the conversion is $1/x$. In the last four lines the proof increases it to $x^{-1+\varepsilon}$. The same $T$ already satisfies the bad-orbit estimate. [Conversion theorem](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/SharesOfCounts.lean#L6-L13) · [Selection, conversion and final weakening](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/topsGroup.lean#L90-L143)

The proof carries these facts through `topsFrame`, `topsBase` and `topsChoiceH`. Having obtained a prime-pair row on $D$, `hostOfFieldOf` uses that very row in `hTops`. It takes the resulting $T$ and returns the updated host `{ D with TT := TT }` with the same set's share certificate. `FReq.at_of_from` later identifies the point's retained set by injectivity of its coordinate map before it transports a host requirement. Equal cardinalities provide insufficient information for that identification. [Host construction](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ShortNorms/hostOfFieldOf.lean#L217-L227) · [Identification of the represented set](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/Supply.lean#L21-L34)

## Why the character bound has the constant four

`QH.pairEx` combines an existence theorem for a pair of primes with `QH.kappa4`. The latter yields $\|L'/L(1,\chi)\|\le4\log Y$ for characters satisfying `GoodC Q χ` and lower and upper size bounds on $\log Q$, beyond a threshold depending on the tolerance and the fixed growth parameter. The pair proof takes that parameter to be 3, constructs the good-character hypotheses from the pair, and uses $Y\le y$ to obtain $4\log y$ everywhere required. The factor four and the underlying zero-free hypotheses come from upstream analytic estimates. [Logarithmic-derivative bound](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/MeanSquareBounds/Kappa4Loglog.lean#L126-L141) · [Prime selection and uniform evaluation](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/MeanSquareBounds/pairEx.lean#L112-L164)

`boundedTw_key` represents each bounded primitive character by a twist with at least one nonzero twist index. `boundedTw` reads the two components of `PairGood.bounds` for that character at $y=x$. At lines 125–128 of `supplyOfAdmOf`, `hSh` from the chosen host and these two estimates are inserted directly into `EAtH`. This pins down which source constructor the cyclotomic path uses. The earlier comparison with `PreRows.eAtH` illustrated another valid constructor whose range conversion yields $\kappa.C\,J$. The actual cyclotomic path uses 4. [Twist representation and evaluation](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/BoundedTw.lean#L45-L56) · [Construction of EAtH](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ShortNorms/supplyOfAdmOf.lean#L110-L131)

The downstream theorem `charsA` uses shares for the Fourier estimate on retained nontrivial characters. Retained log derivatives are charged to the fixed level sum `koOf Λ`. For an unretained character in the comparison class it obtains boundedness and reads `bdL` for its primitive representative. The prime bound also uses `gmA_eq`, `sigA_le`, the core, and the remaining error estimates. Sharpening one Fourier estimate establishes an intermediate improvement. A smaller exponent in the final bound would require tracing the competing errors.
