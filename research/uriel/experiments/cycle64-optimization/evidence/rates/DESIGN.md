# Fixed-support rate experiment

This is a follow-up to the frozen 64-residue support decision in [study 14](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/d67de1f6716496b98c8f0eb9f16b2a38c4f789c4/research/uriel/experiments/ten-percent-study/evidence/alternative/AUDIT.md). Hold its support selection, coordinate normalization and dense six-leaf deletion rule fixed. The labeling is known before assigning rates, and each finite profile may prescribe a different positive rate on every occupied leaf. The rate vector must sum to 64.

The limiting marked-tile factor above 1/10 certificates, with all original interval bounds, are the sufficient class considered here. We first examine profiles independent of the source parameter epsilon over its allowed interval. A sensitivity panel permits rates to depend on epsilon. The upper bounds apply to these certificate classes. Membership in them is sufficient for passing the underlying score.

Exploratory arithmetic identified the homogeneously mixed leaf and the rare singleton mark as candidate active constraints. The following checks were specified after this exploration and before the exact replay. Check the feasible rational profile slightly inside the proposed intersection, an infeasible control for each active boundary, the normalized gain against direct differences of the prior tight labeling, and a linear certificate excluding improvements even under unequal individual leaf rates. Check all other branches using the prior unit-surplus bounds with an independent rate and denominator estimate. Compare the same three-rate grouping with an unrestricted per-leaf assignment on the same support. A feasible point alone will only be counted as a lower bound.

The dimensionless tile factor and source integer are taken from the [published mark](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SubspaceCounting/GoodOrbitIntervalTileCondition.lean#L12-L17) and [published scale definitions](https://github.com/nousentllc/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThickFrames/MarkedLow.lean#L23-L29). The finite script has no external dependencies.
