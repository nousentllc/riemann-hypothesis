## How the two implementations meet

For a fixed support let u_t equal twice the sum of missing odd step counts in ancestors where leaf t is canonical odd. Define k_t=1+sum of the widths of those ancestors. At A/C the surplus coefficient is C u_t−A k_t. The best closed box pays 12/5 to the top 26 coefficients and 8/5 to the next. Integer surpluses use rates multiplied by five.

For the base envelope award zero root credit and record the best contribution in each 32-leaf half by the number of full and partial rate slots. Maximize over the 54 possible splits, charging the canonical odd half the width-64 cost. The optimized implementation computes each sub-envelope recursively through its two 16-leaf quarters, then restores credit to geometries that can earn missing-generator gain at width 32. The other sector explicitly visits supports with an absent root generator and occupied roots of both parities. A chosen preferred half forbids both translates by the specified generator on the opposite half. Taking the largest admissible interior removes every leaf that requires a missing preferred minimum. Inspect only viable combinations of quarter submasks, deduplicate on the first absent unsigned generator and sort actual coefficients.

The independent implementation directly enumerates 3,263,443 possible supports on either half, computing missing steps by rotations. It handles the root-hole sector using a global include/exclude search with adjacency constraints. Every selected leaf requires its ancestor minima. Banning a prerequisite propagates to all leaves that depend on it. The output agrees on 36,802 support/hole pairs, 33,631 distinct supports and sector maxima of 0 and −210,792 at 99/916. A third, literal Python calculation subtracts the winning residues and reproduces 1584/14656. All 27 rates are positive; residue 47 takes 8/5.

The optional search driver reproduces the exploratory initial scan and its 12 rounds of local mutations. The default verification uses the saved adversarial word. The manuscript explains the role of quantifiers and why this upper witness cannot identify the original retained set.
