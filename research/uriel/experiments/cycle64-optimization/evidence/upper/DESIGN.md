# Upper-bound panel

The primary question is a guaranteed upper bound on the worst-labeling optimum of the 64-residue stationary profile class with fixed positive supported rates at most 12/5, normalized to sum 64, and realized edges wherever the periodic support admits an orientation. Use a specified adverse labeling and relax the positive support rates to a closed box, evaluating the old support geometry. Retain the necessary nonempty preferred child at every occupied node and relax the quantitative tile probabilities. The box vertex has 26 full rates 12/5 and one partial rate 8/5. A proof of an upper threshold requires a complete bound in every support sector. Track held input labeling and every tested threshold, finite arithmetic and a lower witness of the relaxation. A failure of one proposed labeling to give a close upper bound is an experimental result.

Partition the geometry into the contribution from below the root and a sector with at least one missing root generator. For the first, optimize the two 32-leaf branches independently for every split of full-rate and partial-rate slots. For the second, condition on the smallest missing unsigned generator and remove forbidden translates before enumerating the other half. Test the extraction and maximizing vertex by direct modular differences. A sampled maximizing support can supply only a lower bound in the relaxation. A capped-profile upper bound alone cannot bound the unrestricted arithmetic selector.
